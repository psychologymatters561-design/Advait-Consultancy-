## What happens next. {After}

If our way of working fits yours, the next step is a Ground Session: 60 to 75 minutes on your land, at your premises, or by video. The fee is ₹15,000, credited in full against any mandate.

If it does not fit, we will say so, and point you to the research we publish. There is no follow-up and no pitch.

If you are holding a problem with land, you may also simply bring it to us. We work it until we find the best answer, including when the best answer is to do nothing.
