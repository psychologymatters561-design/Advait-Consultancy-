## When families call us. {The problem first}

Families call about a problem, not a product. Land without a plan. A developer at the door. Heirs who don't agree. A permission that won't move.

Bring us the problem. We work it until we find the best answer, including when the best answer is to do nothing.

## The six services. {Services}

!services

Every service rests on the same method: read what reaches the land, secure what it may become, and design what it should become. [Read the approach](/approach/).

## How we are paid. {Fees}

We are paid only by the client. Never by a seller, a builder or a developer.

!fees

## What we will not do. {Limits}

- We do not sell, list, price or promote any developer's project or unit.
- We do not take a fee from the other side of any transaction.
- We do not give legal or tax advice. We work alongside your lawyers and accountants.
- We do not promise returns, and we do not design for quick exits.

Land can return capital early, and sometimes it does. We simply don't design for it. If the money must come back within two years, we are not the right counsel — and we'll say so.

!cta
