The Advisory

Four principles.

01 — The Practice

We structure what others only transact.

The value of an asset is not fixed at listing. It is determined by how the entry is structured, when it is timed, and what is built around it. Advisory changes the outcome before the deal is signed.

02 — The Principle

One client. One deal. No conflict.

We do not represent both sides of a transaction. We do not work for builders while advising buyers. When we take a mandate, it is singular. That singularity is worth more than any fee.

03 — The Method

We think with everything.

Infrastructure pipelines. Policy sequencing. Capital flow direction. Historical land-use patterns. Market sentiment. Regulatory trajectory. None of these exist in isolation. Neither does the asset.

04 — The Network

Some opportunities are never listed.

They are placed. By the time an asset reaches a portal, its best entry point has already passed. We work at the layer before that.

India is building at a scale history will measure in decades, not quarters.

Infrastructure supercycle · 2024–2040

93% of India's next consumer-class expansion will occur outside the five largest cities. Infrastructure is moving ahead of population. The opportunity is in the gap between the two.

Policy, not sentiment, drives land value in India's corridors. An expressway announcement is not news — it is a pricing event, months before the market reflects it. Understanding the pipeline is understanding the map.

We operate in that window — between the policy signal and the market's response to it.

Advait Consultancy is a New Delhi-based independent real estate advisory firm specialising in infrastructure-aligned investments in India. Founded on the principle that real estate value is structured before entry rather than discovered at listing, the firm provides fee-based advisory, due diligence, and deal-structuring services to investors and developers across Delhi NCR, including the Jewar aerotropolis and YEIDA corridor adjacent to the upcoming Noida International Airport. Advait Consultancy works on single-client mandates and does not represent both parties in any transaction.

Individually, capital moves.
Collectively, it shapes.

Capital is not the scarce resource. Aligned capital is.

Every serious investor in India has money. Most of them have networks. Almost none of them have access to a structure where both move together, at the speed that early-stage corridors require.

What we are building.

A small, closed network of individuals who bring capital to a structure — but also their relationships, their information, and their intent to build something that outlasts the investment. Each member of this network expands its surface — the deals it can see, the speed at which it can move, and the influence it accumulates as its ownership of India's corridors grows. What one person's capital cannot reach alone, this network reaches together — not because the money pools, but because the intelligence does. India's infrastructure buildout is real. The question is only: whose capital builds it, and whose capital collects its returns. We believe those should be the same people.

How it works.

Each deployment is ring-fenced. Ownership is proportional, documented, and legally enforceable. This is not a fund, and it is not a pool of trust. It is a structure where your position is yours — independent of every other member's position. What is shared is not the money. It is the advantage of combined intelligence.
