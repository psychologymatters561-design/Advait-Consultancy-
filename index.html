KM 058 · The advisory

Four principles.

01The Practice

We structure what others only transact.

The value of an asset is not fixed at listing. It is determined by how the entry is structured, when it is timed, and what is built around it. Advisory changes the outcome before the deal is signed.

02The Principle

One client. One deal. No conflict.

We do not represent both sides of a transaction. We do not work for builders while advising buyers. When we take a mandate, it is singular. That singularity is worth more than any fee.

03The Method

We think with everything.

Infrastructure pipelines. Policy sequencing. Capital flow direction. Historical land-use patterns. Market sentiment. Regulatory trajectory. Across Gurugram, Noida, and the expressway corridors, none of these exist in isolation. Neither does the asset.

04The Network

KM 096 · The thesis

India is building at a scale history will measure in decades, not quarters.

Infrastructure supercycle · 2024–2040

93% of India's next consumer-class expansion will occur outside the five largest cities. Infrastructure is moving ahead of population. The opportunity is in the gap between the two.

Policy, not sentiment, drives land value in India's corridors. An expressway announcement is not news. It is a pricing event, months before the market reflects it. Understanding the pipeline is understanding the map.

We operate in that window, between the policy signal and the market's response to it. That is where advisory becomes advantage.

Questions, plainly answered
What is infrastructure-aligned real estate advisory?+

It is the practice of reading real estate through the infrastructure moving toward it — expressways, transit, airports, and the policy that sequences them. In India's corridors, value is set by what is built around an asset and when. We advise on the entry before that value is visible to the market.

How is Advait Consultancy different from a broker?+

A broker is paid to close a transaction and often represents both sides of it. Advait Consultancy is paid for counsel and takes a single-client mandate on one side only. We are not compensated by builders or developers, and we never represent both parties to a deal.

What does “single-client mandate” mean?+

We work for one client, on one deal, with no competing interest on the other side. That singularity is the point. It is what keeps the counsel independent and the alignment complete.

Which markets across Delhi NCR does Advait Consultancy work in?+

New Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad and Sohna, along the Dwarka Expressway and the Yamuna Expressway toward Jewar. We work wherever infrastructure is moving ahead of the market across Delhi NCR.

What is Advait Capital?+

Advait Capital is a closed circle beyond the advisory practice, for a deliberately small number of aligned investors. It is entered by recognition, not application. Its workings are discussed only with those who reach out.
